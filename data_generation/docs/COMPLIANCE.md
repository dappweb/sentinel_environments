# Compliance

## Synthetic Data
Benchmark catalogs and generated benchmark media are AI-generated. They do not
contain real users, PII, or scraped content.

Author headshots under `data/author-photos/` are real contributor photos
used for project/landing-page attribution only. They are not benchmark catalog
data and are not produced by the synthetic data pipeline.
